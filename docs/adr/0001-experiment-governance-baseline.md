# Establish the recommendation-experiment governance baseline

On 2026-08-01, AssetTrack fixed Cross-model outcomes to QQQ, set point-in-time research and truth retention to two years, chose globally shared Policy Definitions with per-user Champion assignments and ledgers, limited automatic Candidates to small whitelisted one-parameter proposals, and selected Brier skill for probability-policy Promotion plus benchmark-adjusted signed return for direction-only Promotion. These choices keep comparisons consistent and auditable while preventing either a user-specific ledger or an automatic diagnostic from silently redefining the underlying policy; Candidate application and every Promotion remain separately validated and user-controlled.
